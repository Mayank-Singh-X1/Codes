Pro-Tips for the Assessment:
For the Portfolio: Remember that when you use grid-column: span 2, you need to ensure the total number of columns in your grid-template-columns accommodates it (e.g., a 3-column or 4-column base).

For Responsiveness: Start with your Desktop styles, then use @media (max-width: 1024px) for tablets and @media (max-width: 768px) for mobile.

For the Overlay: Ensure the parent portfolio item has position: relative so your position: absolute overlay stays contained within the card.
